🧑‍🎓

Refill Buddy — a digital prototype for discovering campus water refill points.

Project details

University: Lovely Professional University (LPU)

Course: INT335 Design Thinking

Component: CP5 • Project P16

Focus: Help students discover and use water refill points on campus.

Problem & opportunity

Students may not know where refill points are, whether a point is usable, or how to reach it. A simple campus guide could make refilling more convenient and encourage reusable bottles.

Prototype features

• Home page and quick actions

• Explore points on a sample map

• Point details and demo status

• Step-by-step directions

• Report an issue form

• Project overview and testing journey

Suggested student testing journey

  1. Open the app and understand its purpose.
  2. Find the Explore section.
  3. Locate a refill point on the map.
  4. Check its details and sample status.
  5. Follow the directions.
  6. Try the report form and share feedback.

Interview questions

1. Was it easy to find a refill point?

2. Were the location details clear?

3. Did the status help you decide?

4. Were the directions easy to follow?

5. What would you improve?

6. Would you use this app on campus? Why?

Limitations & next steps

All map locations, routes and status labels are illustrative placeholders, not verified LPU data. A real version needs verified locations, live status updates, student testing, accessibility checks and a working reporting backend. Water quality must be checked separately by the responsible authority.
